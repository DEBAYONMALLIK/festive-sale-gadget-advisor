Read this before you trust a number

Prices move

Everything is read live from public product pages and can change the moment a sale wave starts. Always confirm on the listing before you pay.

Bank offers excluded

Card and bank discounts are listed separately and are never folded into the headline price, because eligibility varies per shopper.

Captchas happen

Marketplaces challenge shared datacenter IPs. When a lookup is blocked the answer says the price is unverified rather than guessing one.

Every claim is cited

Recommendations link the Reddit threads and YouTube reviews they came from, so you can check the reasoning yourself.
